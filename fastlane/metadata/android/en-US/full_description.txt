Eco is a warm, thoughtful assistant for questions about nature, climate and caring for the living world.

Everything runs on your phone:
<ul>
<li>No account, no server, no tracking, no ads.</li>
<li>Nothing you type leaves your phone.</li>
<li>After a one-time download of its files, Eco works offline.</li>
</ul>

Eco answers from a built-in library of trusted environmental information. It looks up the most relevant passages for each question and answers only from them. If its sources don't cover a question, it says so instead of guessing.

<b>First start</b>
On first start Eco shows how much it needs to download (about 3.5 GB: the Eco language model and its knowledge index) and waits for you to tap Download. Wi-Fi only is on by default. The download runs in the background and resumes if interrupted. Each file is checked against its published SHA-256 checksum before use.

You can also copy the files to the phone yourself, for example after downloading them on a computer, and choose them in Settings.

<b>Requirements</b>
A 64-bit ARM phone with at least 8 GB of RAM and about 4 GB of free storage. Eco uses the phone's GPU where possible (OpenCL on Snapdragon/Adreno, Vulkan elsewhere) and otherwise the CPU.

Eco is built on llama.cpp and the Qwen3-4B model.
